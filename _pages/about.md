# Jinsuk Baek, Ph.D.

I am a Professor of Computer Science at Winston-Salem State University (WSSU). 
My research focuses on intelligent and networked computing systems, with current 
interests spanning quantum networking and computing, intelligent robotic systems, 
and Internet of Things (IoT) technologies.

My research explores the design of reliable, adaptive, and intelligent systems 
that integrate communication, computing, sensing, and physical interaction. 
Current projects include quantum communication and networking, robotic systems 
for smart environments, and adaptive communication protocols for IoT applications.

## Research Interests

- **Quantum Networking and Computing**
- **Intelligent Robotic Systems**
- **Internet of Things (IoT) and Smart Environments**
- **Computer Networks and Adaptive Communication Systems**

## Current Research

### Quantum Networking and Computing
Research on quantum communication networks, quantum repeater systems, 
fidelity-aware routing, and practical quantum computing applications.

### Intelligent Robotic Systems
Development of intelligent robotic systems with an emphasis on perception, 
coordination, synchronization, and reliable manipulation in smart environments.

### IoT and Networked Intelligent Systems
Design of adaptive communication and networking mechanisms for heterogeneous 
IoT systems, including task-aware and cross-protocol communication.

## Research Projects

My current and recent research activities include federally funded quantum 
information science projects and international collaborative research on 
intelligent robotic and smart-kitchen systems.

## Publications

My research has been published in IEEE journals and other peer-reviewed venues 
in computer networking, quantum information science, IoT, and intelligent 
robotic systems.
