My research focuses on the design of reliable, adaptive, and intelligent
computing systems that integrate communication, computing, sensing, and
physical interaction. My current research spans quantum networking and
computing, intelligent robotic systems, and IoT and networked intelligent
systems.

## Quantum Networking and Computing

![Quantum Networking and Computing](/images/Figure1.png)

My research in quantum networking and computing focuses on reliable and
efficient quantum communication and computing systems. Current interests
include quantum repeater networks, fidelity-aware routing, adaptive quantum
network control, and practical quantum computing.

## Intelligent Robotic Systems

  <img src="/images/Figure2.png" alt="Intelligent Robotic Systems" width="75%">

My research in intelligent robotic systems focuses on reliable perception,
coordination, and manipulation in dynamic physical environments. Current
topics include dual-arm robotic systems, RGB-D vision, synchronization,
adaptive control, and robotic cooking.

## IoT and Networked Intelligent Systems

![IoT and Networked Intelligent Systems](/images/Figure3.png)

My research in IoT and networked intelligent systems investigates reliable
and adaptive communication mechanisms for heterogeneous networked
environments. Current interests include IoT communication, adaptive
networking protocols, MQTT and CoAP, network resilience, and task-aware
communication.
